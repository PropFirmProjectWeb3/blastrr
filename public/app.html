Standard purchase discount

This percentage applies to all plan purchases. Battle-pass reward codes add their discount to this standard rate, capped at 100%.

XP and points per plan purchased

Awards are credited once after each verified paid purchase. Free 100%-off reward-code redemptions do not grant purchase XP or points.

Level discount rewards

All twelve levels unlock at 10,000 XP intervals, up to 120,000 XP. Admins set each reward's additional discount and point cost.

Fixed activity rewards

Green day and week rewards unlock after profitable UTC periods close. Trade-a-day unlocks after a closed trade; paid-withdrawal unlocks when a payout is marked paid. Admins set XP and points for these fixed tasks.

Custom social tasks

Create ad hoc social tasks with daily, weekly, or monthly limits and an X post URL to embed. Users earn the reward immediately when opening the task link.
