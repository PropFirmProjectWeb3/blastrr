About Blastrr

Blastrr is a crypto-native, wallet-first simulated trading terminal built for modern digital asset traders. With instant Web3 wallet onboarding, USDT plan payments, static drawdown rules, and 24/7 access to 20 major crypto markets, we give traders a streamlined, transparent environment to prove their edge.

We are expanding our creator ecosystem and looking for a proactive, crypto-savvy Creator Partnerships & Affiliate Specialist to scout, pitch, and onboard trading influencers across social media.

The role

You will be the frontline recruiter for Blastrr’s Creator Program. Your core mission is to find high-converting crypto and derivatives trading creators on X (Twitter), YouTube, TikTok, Discord, and Telegram, pitch them on partnering with Blastrr, and guide them through onboarding so leadership can assign their custom referral commissions and promo codes.

What you’ll do

  • Outbound creator scouting: Prospect across X, YouTube, TikTok, and Telegram to identify micro- and mid-tier crypto traders, chart analysts, and prop-trading educators with engaged, authentic audiences.
  • Cold outreach and pitching: Craft tailored DMs, emails, and proposals highlighting wallet-first access, 24/7 crypto markets, static drawdown, and bespoke revenue-share tiers.
  • Vetting and qualification: Look beyond vanity follower counts—check engagement, audience quality, past prop-firm or exchange promotions, and brand safety before recommending custom commission tiers.
  • Seamless onboarding: Guide approved creators through setting up their Blastrr Creator Profile, generating referral codes, and submitting commission requests to admin.
  • Pipeline management and reporting: Maintain an organized CRM or tracker for outreach volume, response rates, onboarded creators, and referred plan volume.
  • Creator retention: Check in with creators, share promotional assets or free-trial account codes when approved by leadership, and help them maximize conversions.

What you bring

  • Crypto and trading fluency: You know spot versus perpetuals, understand prop-trading rules such as drawdowns and profit targets, and speak the language of Crypto Twitter and trading communities.
  • Outreach experience: At least one year in affiliate recruitment, influencer marketing, Web3 business development, or high-volume outbound sales.
  • Persuasive communication: Strong written English and the ability to write punchy, non-spammy DMs that get replies from busy creators.
  • An analytical eye: You can spot fake engagement and bot-driven accounts and identify smaller creators whose audiences convert.
  • A self-starter mindset: You are comfortable working 100% remotely with clear daily and weekly outreach KPIs and minimal hand-holding.

Nice to have

  • An existing network of crypto traders, signal-group owners, or Web3 KOLs.
  • Familiarity with Web3 wallets such as MetaMask, Rabby, and Phantom, and stablecoin ecosystems including USDT across EVM chains.
  • Multilingual skills—especially Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Vietnamese, or Korean—to reach international crypto trading communities.
