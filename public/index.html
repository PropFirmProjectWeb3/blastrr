Know the rules

Trade the plan.
Protect the downside.

Know your limits before the first position.

Daily loss limit — 5%

The daily loss limit is 5% of the plan's daily starting equity. A breach locks the account for 24 hours; when the lock expires, the daily starting equity resets to the then-current equity.

Maximum drawdown — 10% static

The maximum loss limit is 10% of the plan's initial simulated balance and does not trail profits. Breaching it permanently locks the account.

Profit target

Each plan has a stated target equal to 20% of simulated capital. Reaching a target does not by itself guarantee a payout or funded/live brokerage account; eligibility and review conditions apply.

News trading

News trading is prohibited under the stated rules. Review the complete policy for restricted events, time windows, open positions and enforcement.

Questions before you launch?

Review the basics here, then check the complete rules in the app before purchasing a plan.

What does “straight to trading” mean?

You choose and purchase a plan rather than completing a multi-stage evaluation challenge first. Account activation remains subject to payment confirmation and the platform's terms.

Can I buy more than one account?

The stated model allows users to purchase multiple accounts within their budget. Check current account limits, prohibited behaviour and any aggregation rules in the app.

Which token can I use to pay?

USDT only, on a supported network: Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Avalanche or NullNet. Network availability and contract addresses should be confirmed inside the app before sending.

How do withdrawals work?

Withdrawals are stated to be in USDT on supported networks. Timing, minimum amounts, fees, eligibility and any reward split are governed by the current withdrawal policy. Review it before you trade.

Do crypto markets close?

Crypto markets generally trade 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, without the scheduled daily sessions and weekly closes typical of many FX and futures markets. Individual exchanges or the Blastrr platform may have maintenance or service interruptions, and continuous trading does not mean prices or liquidity are constant.

How is the standard plan discount applied?

The current standard discount is applied automatically to plan prices. BlastrrPass reward codes can add an additional discount at checkout, up to 100% off in total.
